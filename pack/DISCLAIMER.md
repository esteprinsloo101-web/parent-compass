- **Not** an AI therapist and not a chat “counsellor.”
- **Not** medical, legal, or child-protection advice.
- **Not** for children to use unsupervised as a clinical tool.

## Abuse, violence, or a child in danger

If there is abuse, violence, exploitation, or a child is in danger:

1. Call emergency services — **112**
2. Call the police / SAPS — **10111** (when police response is needed)
3. Call **Childline South Africa — 116** (toll-free, 24 hours, free from all networks; cite: [childlinesa.org.za](https://www.childlinesa.org.za/contact-us/))
4. Or find local help via [findahelpline.com](https://findahelpline.com)

Do **not** rely on this pack instead of reporting or seeking protection.
