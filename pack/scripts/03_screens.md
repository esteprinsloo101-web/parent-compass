# Phone / screen time

**Parent Compass script 03/08** · Adults 18+ · Not therapy

> If abuse, violence, or a child in danger: call **112**, SAPS **10111**, Childline SA **116**, or use https://findahelpline.com

## What's going on

Screens are designed to hook attention. Battles often spike at transitions (school → home, evening wind-down) and when the rule feels sudden or inconsistent.

## Try this

1. Agree house rules in calm time (not mid-scroll): where, when, and max blocks.
2. Use a visible timer or “two more minutes then park the phone.”
3. Offer a ready alternative (snack, outdoors, board game, shower) before the cut-off.
4. Model the same rule for adults where possible (family phone basket at dinner).
5. For older kids: discuss sleep, homework, kindness online — then keep the limit.

## Why it helps

Clear expectations reduce surprise meltdowns. Transitions need buffering. Shared family norms feel less like targeting one child. Consistency beats perfect speeches.

## Ethics note

You are responsible for a safe digital environment, not for being popular. Respect privacy age-appropriately, but safety overrides secrecy if there is grooming, bullying, or harmful content.

## When to get outside help

If you suspect online exploitation, severe cyberbullying, self-harm content, or addictive patterns harming school/sleep/mood, contact school, SAPS (10111) if crime is involved, Childline 116, or findahelpline.com. Emergency: 112.
