# School stress

**Parent Compass script 07/08** · Adults 18+ · Not therapy

> If abuse, violence, or a child in danger: call **112**, SAPS **10111**, Childline SA **116**, or use https://findahelpline.com

## What's going on

Stress can look like stomach-aches, irritability, avoidance, perfectionism, or shutdown. Causes range from workload and friendships to learning gaps, bullying, or sleep debt.

## Try this

1. Listen first: “What feels heaviest today?” before fixing.
2. Break the load: one subject, one page, one bag packed tonight.
3. Protect sleep and a short buffer after school before homework when possible.
4. Partner with the teacher early — share observations, ask one clear question.
5. Celebrate process (“you started even when it felt hard”) not only marks.

## Why it helps

Feeling heard lowers threat. Chunking reduces overwhelm. Sleep and routine support learning. Early school contact prevents small gaps becoming crises. Process praise builds resilience.

## Ethics note

A child’s worth is not their report mark. Avoid comparing siblings publicly. Do not excuse cheating. Advocate fairly — and also teach responsibility for effort.

## When to get outside help

Involve the school, learning support, or a registered professional if stress includes self-harm talk, bullying that does not stop, sudden grade collapse, or persistent school refusal. Emergency / harm: 112 · Childline 116.
