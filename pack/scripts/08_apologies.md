# Apologies / repair

**Parent Compass script 08/08** · Adults 18+ · Not therapy

> If abuse, violence, or a child in danger: call **112**, SAPS **10111**, Childline SA **116**, or use https://findahelpline.com

## What's going on

Forced “say sorry” often produces empty words. Real repair is naming harm, owning it, and making amends. Kids need coaching — and adults who repair too.

## Try this

1. Cool down first if anyone is flooded; repair lands better after.
2. Coach a real apology: what happened + how it affected the other + what you’ll do next.
3. Add a repair action: replace, help, write a note, give space, try again kindly.
4. Accept a good-faith repair; do not demand theatrical emotion.
5. When you mess up as a parent, apologise briefly and specifically.

## Why it helps

Repair restores trust faster than punishment alone. Practising ownership builds conscience and empathy. Adult apologies teach that power does not cancel responsibility.

## Ethics note

Do not force physical affection as “proof” of sorry. Do not make the harmed child soothe the harm-doer. Justice and kindness can coexist — consequences may still apply after repair.

## When to get outside help

If harm is severe (assault, ongoing intimidation, sexual harm), prioritise safety and report through proper channels (SAPS 10111, social services, Childline 116). Emergency: 112. Parent Compass is not a mediation or legal service.
