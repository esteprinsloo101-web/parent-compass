# Respect / manners

**Parent Compass script 06/08** · Adults 18+ · Not therapy

> If abuse, violence, or a child in danger: call **112**, SAPS **10111**, Childline SA **116**, or use https://findahelpline.com

## What's going on

Manners are social skills. Rudeness can be tiredness, copying peers/media, testing limits, or a child who has not been taught the words yet. Respect is mutual — not fear.

## Try this

1. Model the tone you want: calm voice, please/thank you, listening without interrupting.
2. Teach the phrase: “Try that again with respect.” Then wait for the redo.
3. Praise specific manners when you see them (“Thanks for waiting your turn”).
4. Correct privately when possible; avoid shaming in front of others.
5. Hold the boundary on name-calling and insults — even when emotions run hot.

## Why it helps

Children copy what they live with. Rehearsal (“try again”) builds muscle memory. Private correction protects dignity. Clear no-insult rules keep the home psychologically safer.

## Ethics note

Respect includes culture and elders — and also the child’s dignity. Demanding obedience through fear is not the same as teaching manners. Adults should apologise when they are rude too.

## When to get outside help

If aggression, threats, or intimidation dominate the home (either direction), get outside support. Abuse or danger: 112 · Childline SA 116 · findahelpline.com.
