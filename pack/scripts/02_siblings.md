# Sibling conflict

**Parent Compass script 02/08** · Adults 18+ · Not therapy

> If abuse, violence, or a child in danger: call **112**, SAPS **10111**, Childline SA **116**, or use https://findahelpline.com

## What's going on

Siblings compete for attention, space, and fairness. Conflict can be normal skill-building — unless it turns into bullying, intimidation, or someone is unsafe.

## Try this

1. Pause the scene: separate briefly if voices/bodies escalate.
2. Name what you saw without picking a villain first: “I hear two upset people.”
3. Coach turn-taking or a concrete fix (timer, shared vs personal items).
4. Ask each child: “What do you need next?” then “What can you offer?”
5. Praise repair attempts more than “who won.”

## Why it helps

Coaching problem-solving builds social skills better than endless refereeing. Separating lowers fight-or-flight so brains can think. Fairness often means meeting needs, not identical outcomes.

## Ethics note

Every child deserves safety and dignity. Do not force a hug or apology that is not sincere. Protect the younger/vulnerable child from intimidation while still teaching the older one responsibility.

## When to get outside help

Get outside help if there is ongoing bullying, physical harm, sexualised behaviour, or one child lives in fear. Contact school support, a social worker, or Childline SA 116. Emergency: 112.
